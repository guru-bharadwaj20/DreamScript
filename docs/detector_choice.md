## Verdict

- Spread across all three arms on mAP@0.5 is **0.0142** - the architecture choice is worth less than a hundredth on the seven annotated shape classes, every one of which every arm puts above 0.98.
- Most accurate: **`rtdetr-l`** at 0.9055, and it earns that almost entirely on `arrowhead` (0.486 against `yolov8n`'s 0.345).
- Cheapest: **`yolov8n`** at 0.8913, 5.8x less training time and 10.6x fewer parameters.
- Per-page latency separates the arms far less than training cost does, so the trade is about the retraining cadence Phase 15 sets, not about what a user waits for.
