# Collection Brief — Circuit

**Target: 40 sketches** across 10 scenarios and 8 scribes.

Generated by `scripts/make_collection_sheets.py` from `src/ingest/collection.py`. Do not edit by hand.

## How to draw these

Standard symbols, drawn freehand. Close the loop. Do not use a ruler. Wires crossing without a junction dot must stay unmarked — telling a crossing from a junction is exactly what Phase 10.1.7 has to learn.

## Scenarios

| # | Scenario | What to draw | Must contain |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Series resistors** (`series_resistors`) | Battery and three resistors in a single loop. | closed loop, battery symbol, three resistors |
| 2 | **Parallel resistors** (`parallel_resistors`) | Battery with two resistor branches in parallel. | junction nodes, parallel branches |
| 3 | **RC low-pass filter** (`rc_filter`) | Source, resistor, capacitor to ground, output node. | capacitor symbol, ground symbol |
| 4 | **LED with series resistor** (`led_circuit`) | Battery, resistor, LED, closed loop. | diode symbol |
| 5 | **Voltage divider** (`voltage_divider`) | Two resistors between supply and ground with a tapped midpoint. | labelled midpoint |
| 6 | **AND gate** (`and_gate`) | Two inputs into an AND gate, one output. | gate symbol, two inputs |
| 7 | **Half adder** (`half_adder`) | XOR and AND gates producing sum and carry. | two gates, two outputs |
| 8 | **SR latch** (`sr_latch`) | Two cross-coupled NOR gates. | feedback loop, two gates |
| 9 | **Switch and lamp** (`switch_lamp`) | Battery, switch, lamp in series. | switch symbol, lamp symbol |
| 10 | **Transistor switch** (`transistor_switch`) | Base resistor, NPN transistor, load, supply rail. | transistor symbol, supply rail |
