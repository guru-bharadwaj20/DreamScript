# Collection Brief — Er Diagram

**Target: 50 sketches** across 10 scenarios and 8 scribes.

Generated by `scripts/make_collection_sheets.py` from `src/ingest/collection.py`. Do not edit by hand.

## How to draw these

Rectangles for entities, ovals for attributes, diamonds for relationships. Write the cardinality (1, N, M) on the lines. Underline primary keys. Attribute-heavy diagrams are what teach the Phase 7.2 Naive Bayes prior that many labels means ER.

## Scenarios

| # | Scenario | What to draw | Must contain |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Blog** (`blog`) | User, Post, Comment with one-to-many relationships and key attributes. | three entities, two relationships, cardinality marks |

| 3 | **E-commerce** (`ecommerce`) | Customer, Order, OrderLine, Product. | four entities, composite relationship |
| 4 | **Library** (`library`) | Member, Book, Loan, with due-date attributes. | attribute ovals, one-to-many |
| 5 | **Hospital** (`hospital`) | Patient, Doctor, Appointment, Prescription. | four entities, chained relationships |
| 6 | **Bank** (`bank`) | Customer, Account, Transaction, with a self-referencing transfer. | self-relationship |
| 7 | **Cinema booking** (`cinema`) | Film, Screening, Seat, Booking. | many-to-many, weak entity |
| 8 | **Warehouse inventory** (`inventory`) | Product, Warehouse, Stock with quantity on the relationship. | relationship attribute |
| 9 | **Social network** (`social`) | User with a self-referencing follows relationship, plus Post. | self-relationship, cardinality both ways |
| 10 | **HR** (`hr`) | Employee, Department, Role, with a manager self-link. | self-relationship, three entities |
