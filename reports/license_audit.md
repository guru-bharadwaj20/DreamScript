## Rules this audit enforces

1. **Unknown means no.** A source with an undeclared license is treated as
   non-redistributable until its author confirms otherwise.
2. **Raw files never enter git.** `.gitignore` excludes `data/raw/`; the registry
   records provenance so the corpus can be rebuilt instead of shipped.
3. **Attribution flows into model cards.** Any model trained on an attribution-
   licensed source names that source in its card (Phase 0.3.2).
4. **Unavailable sources stay registered.** Dead datasets keep a record with the
   evidence of their unavailability, so a substitution is never silent.
