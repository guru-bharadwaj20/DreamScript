## The caveat that matters

Rows marked `native_split_no_scribe_id` or `hashed_no_scribe_id` come from sources
that do not publish writer identity, so **they cannot be guaranteed scribe-disjoint**.
Any result claiming cross-writer generalization must be computed on the
`source_writer_split` and `scribe_disjoint` rows only. The column exists so that
filter is one line of code rather than an act of memory.
