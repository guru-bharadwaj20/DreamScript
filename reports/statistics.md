Generated by `python -m src.eval.statistics_`. Sampling uncertainty: percentile bootstrap over items, 10000 resamples, seed 20260922, resampling **items** (pages, crops) rather than characters, because a crop's errors are correlated within the crop. Seed uncertainty: read from the criterion's own repeats; never estimated where absent.
